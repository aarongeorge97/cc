# Body Drafts — FocusForge (run-id: smoketest)

Hook (already approved, 18 words):
> "11 AM. 23 Slack replies. Zero commits. You reach for cmd-tab. Your best four hours are already gone."

Body word budget: **42 words. HARD CEILING.**

Beat plan: pain reinforcement (1 line, since hook did the heavy lifting) → product introduction → key differentiator → proof if length allows.

---

## DRAFT 1

Your productivity app is the distraction now. FocusForge is one button. Click Forge — Slack, Twitter, every tab dies for 90 minutes. Kernel-level. No override. No streaks. No menu-bar icon. You literally cannot reach Twitter.

Word count: 34

### DIAGNOSIS 1

- Beat 1 ("Your productivity app is the distraction now.") lands the angle's diagnosis line — good, it's the named emotional trigger from the Audience Profile.
- Beat 2 ("FocusForge is one button. Click Forge…") introduces the product cleanly. "every tab dies" is slightly metaphorical for a flat brand — devs would say "closes" or "is unreachable."
- Beat 3 (the negation list) is the right structural move per Brand Voice section 4 ("the feature recital"). But "No menu-bar icon" might be one negation too many for a 30-second Reel — viewer can absorb three, four starts to blur.
- Beat 4 ("You literally cannot reach Twitter") is good proof — it's a Brand Voice on-brand phrase verbatim. But it's slightly redundant with the kernel-level claim above it; it restates rather than escalates.
- Weakest line: "Slack, Twitter, every tab dies for 90 minutes" — the "dies" verb has too much warmth/drama for a flat voice. Should be a colder mechanical verb.
- Energy dip: the transition from "FocusForge is one button" to "Click Forge" feels slightly redundant — both are introducing the same action.
- 34/42 words used — 8 words of headroom for proof or sharper specifics.

## DRAFT 2

Your productivity app is the distraction now. FocusForge is one button. Hit Forge and Slack, Twitter, every tab is unreachable for 90 minutes. Kernel-level. No override button to hunt for. No streaks. $39 once. macOS only.

Word count: 35

### DIAGNOSIS 2

- "is unreachable" is colder and more accurate than "dies" — better voice match.
- "No override button to hunt for" earns its place because it directly answers Marcus's #1 objection ("I'll bypass this one too within five minutes") — it's not just a negation, it's a specific refusal that names the exact failure mode of every competitor.
- "$39 once. macOS only." adds proof and answers two more objections (subscription flip, scope discipline) in 4 words. High value-per-word.
- Dropped "No menu-bar icon" — the four remaining negations (override, streaks, $39 once, macOS only) cover the audience's actual fears more efficiently. Menu-bar icon is a nice-to-have, not a deal-maker.
- "Hit Forge" is sharper than "Click Forge" — Brand Voice favors imperative, mechanical verbs.
- Rhythm check: short. fragment. fragment. longer sentence. fragment. fragment. fragment. fragment. fragment. — matches Brand Voice section 4 ("git log of declarations").
- Voice match: no warmth, no exclamation, no aspiration, technical terms unexplained. Good.
- Concern: "Hit Forge and Slack, Twitter, every tab is unreachable" is the longest sentence at 11 words — within 6-12 range but on the edge. Replacing "and" with an em-dash would tighten cadence and respect the Brand Voice rule that the voice cuts rather than connects (em-dash here is a hard cut, not a podcast-host aside, so it's allowed).
- 35/42 — 7 words of headroom. Worth using? The script already lands the angle, the product, the differentiator, and price + scope proof. Adding more would dilute. Leave the headroom — restraint is the personality (Brand Voice 1).
