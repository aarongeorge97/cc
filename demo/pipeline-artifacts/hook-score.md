## Hook 1 — THE BOLD CLAIM

**Text:** 14 focus apps in 6 years. Bypassed every one inside five minutes. The one that held: kernel-level. No override.

| Dimension | Score |
|---|---|
| Scroll-stop power | 9 |
| Specificity | 10 |
| Emotional charge | 9 |
| Voice match | 9 |
| Differentiation | 10 |

**Notes:**
- Scroll-stop (9 -> needs 10): The opener "14 focus apps in 6 years" reads as ad math — it lands as a setup, not a hit. Marcus has seen "I tried X tools" hooks. The thumb-stopping moment is delayed to "Bypassed every one inside five minutes," which is buried as the second beat. Lead with the bypass, not the count.
- Emotional charge (9 -> needs 10): "The one that held" is mildly metaphorical and the brand voice is allergic to soft metaphor. The line is satisfying but does not produce an involuntary reaction the way "no override" alone would.
- Voice match (9 -> needs 10): Two issues. (a) "held me" was diagnosed and changed to "held" but "held" is still soft — the brand voice does not personify tools as things that hold you. (b) The colon construct ("The one that held: kernel-level") is a writerly punctuation move; the voice prefers period-stops, not colon-reveals. Compare Voice Guide's "It's raining. The forecast said it wouldn't. The forecast is a productivity app." — no colons, no setups.

**Verdict: FAIL.**

## Hook 2 — THE PAIN CALL-OUT

**Text:** Your productivity app is the distraction now. Forest emails about your dead streak. Opal pings your focus score. Streaks summarises your week.

| Dimension | Score |
|---|---|
| Scroll-stop power | 10 |
| Specificity | 10 |
| Emotional charge | 9 |
| Voice match | 10 |
| Differentiation | 10 |

**Notes:**
- Emotional charge (9 -> needs 10): The opening line is the diagnosis — flat, perfect, on-brand. The three-competitor list is correct as proof, but the hook ends on the third proof-beat without any turn, payoff, or recognition trigger. A pain call-out that ends on its third example flattens out instead of cresting. Marcus nods at line one, nods at line two, nods at line three, nods at line four — four nods in a row is not the same as one involuntary "yes." The hook needs a closing beat that reframes or indicts, not a third parallel example.

**Verdict: FAIL.**

## Hook 3 — THE CONTRARIAN

**Text:** Streaks are not productivity. They are anxiety with a leaderboard. A focus tool that needs your engagement is not a focus tool.

| Dimension | Score |
|---|---|
| Scroll-stop power | 9 |
| Specificity | 8 |
| Emotional charge | 9 |
| Voice match | 10 |
| Differentiation | 10 |

**Notes:**
- Scroll-stop (9 -> needs 10): The peak is sentence two ("anxiety with a leaderboard"). Sentence three is logical argument, not visceral. The viewer's thumb might already be moving by the third sentence because the hook has resolved its own punch.
- Specificity (8 -> needs 10): Sentence three is principle-language ("a focus tool that needs your engagement"). No concrete object, no named competitor, no number. Compare Hook 2 which names Forest, Opal, Streaks. The contrarian needs a concrete artifact — a dashboard, a streak counter, a notification — not an abstract noun phrase.
- Emotional charge (9 -> needs 10): The second sentence is the share-worthy line per the Audience Profile. The third sentence is reasoned, not felt. The hook peaks at sentence two and tapers, instead of holding the charge.

**Verdict: FAIL.**

## Hook 4 — THE STORY OPENER

**Text:** 11 AM. 23 Slack replies. Zero commits. You reach for cmd-tab. Your best four hours are already gone.

| Dimension | Score |
|---|---|
| Scroll-stop power | 10 |
| Specificity | 10 |
| Emotional charge | 10 |
| Voice match | 10 |
| Differentiation | 10 |

**Notes:**
- Pulled straight from the Audience Profile day-in-the-life. Five fragments, each load-bearing, each on-brand.
- "11 AM. 23 Slack replies. Zero commits." — exact cadence from the Voice Guide ("$39. Once. macOS only.").
- "cmd-tab" is the correct unexplained technical signal.
- "Your best four hours are already gone" is the indictment — present-tense, declarative, no hedge, no resolution. Leaves the viewer mid-recognition rather than at a verdict.
- Differentiation: the indie-hacker Reels feed is full of 5 AM hero stories and $40k MRR launches. A flat, second-person diagnosis of the viewer's actual morning is the opposite move.

**Verdict: PASS.**
