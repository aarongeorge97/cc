Why this change.

  • Subhead opens with what the customer gets, not "We shop…" — same facts, customer POV.
  • Move "first delivery free" into the primary CTA. Cut the hero from 4 CTAs to 2 to remove choice paralysis.

Why this change.

  • Replace supply-side stat (30+ suppliers) with demand-side proof (200+ kitchens served). Visitors care more about who already trusts you.
  • Reframe "0 churned" as a positive achievement by adding the timeframe ("in 12 months"). Same number, completely different read.
  • "0 contracts required" already lives in the value props below — drop the duplicate.
